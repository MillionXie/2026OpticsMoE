Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016/2017 (EuroSAT-SAR authors report this approximate acquisition period; exact paired optical sensing dates are not provided). Sentinel-2 RGB source credited to Copernicus and the EuroSAT authors as released. Original source notices and legal terms retained in licenses/.
Modifications: SAR resampled to paired optical geographic grid, VV/VH fixed radiometric scaling and deterministic three-channel encoding; RGB decoded to lossless PNG; geographic partitioning; common224-pixel model input and train augmentation. No endorsement by the original creators is implied.
The SAR normalization procedure follows the authors' FGMAE reference; its Apache-2.0 source notice is retained.
